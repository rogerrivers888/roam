Shortlist → Settle → Trip

The bit between browsing and the finished day. You shortlist at speed, possibly on the street. Then you sit down with the list, ring places one by one, mark each Booked or Fully booked, drag the survivors into order, see how each candidate sits between the fixed points of the day, set a start time and how long each stop gets, and only when it all fits do you save it to the trip. Nothing you shortlisted is ever lost. Icons throughout are Lucide, which also ships for React Native, so the app can use the same set.

Getting around is decided once, at the top

One control on the shortlist, Car or No car, saved on the trip. With the car, every leg is a driving time and there is nothing else to pick. Without it, each leg shows only the quickest of walking, an Uber and public transport, and you tap the leg if you want to see the other two. Turn-by-turn planning of a leg belongs to the trip, after the day is saved, not to the shortlist.

The same pin with Car chosen. Driving times only, nothing to expand. Short hops between stops in town stay on foot with the car parked, and the card says so.

Statuses a row can have

  • To call: on the list, not yet checked. Phone button live.
  • Booked: swipe right, or tap the chip. Asks for the time, which becomes a fixed point.
  • Fully booked: swipe left. Stays on the list, greyed, dated. Not offered again for this day.
  • No booking needed: parks, markets, galleries. Set from the place type, can be changed.
  • Set aside: your own “not this time”.

Fixed points

  • Home at the start and end, and anything Booked with a time. A selected pin always reports the leg from the previous fixed point and the leg to the next one, with a leave-by time for each.
  • Leave-by is worked back from the fixed time, using the quickest way (or driving, with the car).

Modes

  • Car: driving only. Legs of under ten minutes on foot are shown as walking with the car left parked.
  • No car: quickest of walk, Uber, public transport. Uber time is wait plus ride, with a rough fare.

Step 2 · Settle, then the trip

Only the rows still in the running come through. Set when you leave, give each stop a length, and the arrival times fall out. Fixed points are locked and everything else is worked out around them. When it fits, save it to the trip. Planning a leg in detail, with directions and a hand-off to a maps app, lives on the trip after that.

The trip day after Save, with a leg tapped. The side drawer plans that leg: steps, the other two ways, and a hand-off to a maps app. On a phone the drawer slides in from the right; on a wide screen it is a fixed panel.

Rules in Settle

  • Arrival = previous stop’s end plus the leg. A booked or locked time overrides it and the spare time before it is shown on the leg.
  • Stop length starts from the household pace defaults and is a stepper.
  • Two stops with the same role are flagged. Pick one; the other goes to Not this time.
  • Save stays off while the day overruns or a clash remains. The message names the stop that tips it.

What Save does

  • Writes stops, order, times, lengths and the mode of each leg to the trip day, with booked statuses and times.
  • Everything else stays on the shortlist with its reason and date, visible from the trip day and from the next trip in the same city.

On the trip

  • Every leg can be planned in the drawer. That is where directions and the maps hand-off live, never in the shortlist.
