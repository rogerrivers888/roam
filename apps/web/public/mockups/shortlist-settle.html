Shortlist → Settle → Trip

The bit between browsing and the finished day. You shortlist at speed, possibly on the street. Then you sit down with the list, ring places one by one, mark each Booked or Fully booked, drag the survivors into order, check the travel time between any two, set a start time and how long each stop gets, and only when it all fits do you save it to the trip. Nothing you shortlisted is ever lost: what you did not use stays on the shortlist, with the reason, for another day.

⚙️Settings

Map view of the same shortlist. No dragging here; the list is for that.

Statuses a row can have

  • To call: on the list, not yet checked. Phone button live.
  • Booked: swipe right, or tap the chip. Asks for the time, which becomes a fixed time in Settle.
  • Fully booked: swipe left. Stays on the list, greyed, dated. Not offered for this day again, but kept for the next trip.
  • No booking needed: parks, markets, galleries. Set by the place type, can be changed.
  • Set aside: your own “not this time”. Same as today’s idea, now with a reason.

Things to notice

  • Only Booked, No booking needed and To call rows are “in the running” and go through to Settle.
  • Tap two numbers for the travel time between them, three ways, in either view.
  • Phone numbers are fetched from Google at display time, never stored, per the licensing rule.

Step 2 · Settle: make the times work

Only the rows still in the running come through. Set when you leave, give each stop a length, pick the travel mode for each leg, and the arrival times fall out. Fixed times, such as a booking or the show’s doors, are locked and everything else is calculated around them. When it fits, save it to the trip.

⚙️Settings

The trip day after Save. Committed stops with their booked times; the leftovers stay on the shortlist with their reasons.

Rules in Settle

  • Arrival time = previous stop’s end + the chosen leg. A booked or locked time overrides it and shows the spare time before it.
  • Length per stop starts from the household pace defaults (a meal 1 h, a special activity longer) and is a stepper, so it is one tap to change.
  • Two stops with the same role, such as two dinners, are flagged. You pick one; the other goes to Not this time.
  • Save is disabled while the day overruns or two same-role stops remain. The message names the stop that tips it.

What Save does

  • Writes the stops, order, times and lengths to the trip day. Booked statuses and times go with them.
  • Everything else stays on the shortlist with its reason and date, visible from the trip day and from the next trip in the same city.
