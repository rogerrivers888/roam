Shortlist → Trip

Two steps now, not three. The shortlist is where everything happens: you open a place, read what you need, ring it, mark it booked or not, drag the survivors into order, and watch the times fall into place. When what is left fits the day, you save it. Step 2 is the trip itself, where each leg can be planned in detail. The earlier “Settle the day” screen is gone: it repeated the shortlist with different chrome, so its two useful controls, leave time and stop length, now live on the shortlist and the trip. Icons are Lucide.

Whittled down. Four in the running, all booked or free to walk in, times filled in from the leave time and each stop’s length, and it fits. The four you did not use fold away with their reasons. Save is live.

Two ways to mark a place

  • Swipe when you already know: right is Booked, left is Fully booked.
  • Open it when you need to look first: hours, address, phone, website, menu, reviews, what it means for the family. The booking control at the bottom sets To call / Booked / No booking / Full, and Booked takes a time and party size.

Why there is no Settle step

  • Once a place is Booked it has a time. Once the rest are ordered, every arrival time follows. That is what Settle was showing, so it now shows on the shortlist as you go.
  • Leave time, home-by time and each stop’s length are on the shortlist, and stay editable on the trip after Save.
  • Save is off until nothing is still To call and the day fits. The message says what is blocking it.

The card on the map

  • Always chronological: the previous fixed point, the place, the next fixed point. The window you would have at the place sits on the place.

The same drawer for a walking leg: a small map of the route and the turns. Uber would show the pickup point and a fare, and hand off to the Uber app.

What Save does

  • Writes the stops, order, times, lengths and each leg’s way of travelling to the trip day, with booking status, time and reference.
  • Leftovers stay on the shortlist with their reason, reachable from the trip and from the next trip in the same city.

Directions

  • Fetched when the drawer opens, from Google Routes: walking turns, transit lines and departures, driving. Nothing is stored.
  • Uber: time and fare estimate, hand-off to the Uber app with pickup and drop pre-filled.
  • Hand-offs: Apple Maps, Google Maps, Citymapper.

Changing your mind

  • “Change the plan” reopens the shortlist. The saved day is not touched until you Save again.
