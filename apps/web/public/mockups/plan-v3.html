3 · When. The two-month calendar from Trips, one tap for a day out, two for a stay, and an arrival pill. For a day out the panel shows how long instead: 2 h · 3 h · half a day · all day.

What changes

  • Every row is a control. To is a search box with results; When is the calendar Trips already has, plus arrival or how long; Do and Eat are pills; Budget is pills plus a from–to slider.
  • Fewer rows. Home and the family sit on one line in the header; From and Who appear only when they differ. Five rows, each reading back its choices on one line.
  • Colour. A warm gradient header with the brand trail and pin; an icon in its own colour per row (teal To, amber When, plum Do, coral Eat, green Budget); picture tiles for ideas.
  • Speaking still works on top of all of it and fills the same rows; the checks queue is unchanged.
  • Pills come from the household’s own likes first (browse lists on Household), so nothing needs typing except a place name.
