From the guide, applied
  • One mint field carries the header; everything else is ink on white with 1px rules. No gradients, no coloured shadows.
  • Row icons are Lucide outlines in a single Leaf; no colour-coding of rows.
  • Buttons and selected chips are ink (light) or mint on charcoal (dark). Mint is never a button in light mode.
  • Red belongs to the heart only — the loved tile and the favourite pin.
  • Archivo 800 headings with tight tracking, Archivo body, Caveat for the wordmark alone. 4px corners on controls and tiles; chips are pills.
