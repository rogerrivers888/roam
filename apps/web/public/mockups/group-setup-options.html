Setting a group up — the four questions, three ways

The built screen opens on “Still to chase” when there is nobody to chase and nothing has been asked of anyone (owner, 4 Sep 2026). A group starts empty, and what it needs first is not a progress report — it is four answers: what is mandatory and what is optional, how people get in, how often Roam chases, and what you are charging for. This page draws three shapes for asking them, then works through the money properly — per head or per party, a fixed price or a pot split between whoever comes, a minimum for it to happen at all, and what is refundable — because that is the part with no answer in the app at all today.

Same trip throughout: Cardiff · rugby tour, Fri 13 – Sun 15 November 2026. Roger has hired a coach for £600, holds twenty-six match tickets, and wants a room at the Parkgate and the stadium tour from everybody. Saturday dinner is an optional — he only needs a number for the table.

1 · What is wrong with what is there

Not the layout — the order. The screen that was built is the right screen for week three and the wrong one for minute one.

The four questions, in the owner's order

  • 1 · What is mandatory, what is optional.
  • 2 · The whole invite thing — a code to scan, a link to copy, a WhatsApp group, names.
  • 3 · How often do you want reminders sent.
  • 4 · Is there any part you want to charge for — and everything that hangs off that.

Wizard or scroll?

  • It is the same four things either way. What differs is what happens the second time, when the coach price changes and he wants to touch one of them.
  • Recommendation below: a page that behaves like a wizard on the first run and stays a page afterwards.

WhatsApp, honestly

  • Sharing into a group needs nothing. A link of the form wa.me/?text=… opens WhatsApp with the message written and lets the organiser pick the group. No integration, no account, no review — it can ship with this block.
  • Reminders arriving in WhatsApp is a different thing — the Business API, a verified sender, message templates approved in advance, and a per-message cost. That is the "next" the owner has in mind, and it is weeks not hours.
  • In between: the reminder text is the same either way, so the sender is swappable — one key, no screen changes.

The code

  • Generated from the invite link at the moment it is shown, so a new link is a new code.
  • Large enough to scan across a table, and it is the first thing in the block rather than behind a button.

4 · Block 4 — the money, which is more than an amount

Roger has hired a coach for £600 and holds 26 match tickets at £32 each. Today the app can only be told “£45”, per party, with three words about refunds. Those two costs are not the same shape: the tickets are a price per person that never moves, and the coach is a fixed pot whose price per head falls with every person who joins. The five settings that hang off any of the three

SettingWhat it asksWhy it has to be there
Per head or per party“Priya is bringing Leo — is that two shares or one?”A coach seat is per head; a room is per party; a kitty is usually per party. Getting it wrong is the most common way an organiser ends up short, and the app has no way of saying it today.
A minimum for it to run“It needs 15 heads by Friday 16 October, or it is off.”The owner's question. Every organiser carrying a fixed cost has this number in their head. Stated, it does three things: it is a promise the participant reads before paying, it is the thing the reminders can say (“four more and the coach runs”), and it is what makes cancelling clean.
What happens if it is not metEveryone told, nobody charged, or everyone refunded.Until Roam takes the money, nothing has been taken and the answer is “told, and nobody pays”. When it does, this is the refund rule and it must be written before a card is entered.
Refundable, or until when“Refundable until 16 October — after that I have paid the coach company.”Already in the app as three words with no date behind them. The date is the whole meaning.
When it is dueThe same date as everything else, or its own.Money and bookings do not always share a deadline: the coach deposit is due before anybody books a room.
Later A different price for children“Leo is 15 — a coach seat is a seat, but the museum is half.”A third axis, and the first thing a school or a club will ask for. Not v1; the model should not make it impossible.

Money is a list

  • The coach and the tickets are two charges with two shapes. One “fee” per group cannot describe this trip, and this trip is the ordinary case.
  • Each line: what it is for in the organiser's words, how it is priced, per head or per party, a minimum, a refund rule, a date.
  • The group's total is then a real number — “you are owed £1,368, and £702 of it has reached you”.

5 · What the Group tab says at each stage

The complaint in one line: the screen only ever had one state, and it was the third one. There are four, and only two of them are about chasing.

1 · Nothing set up“Is anyone else coming?” — one card, and the four blocks underneath it, block 1 open. The word chase does not appear.
2 · Set up, nobody inThe code, big, with “nobody has joined yet — send it to them”. The four blocks fold to a line each. Still no chasing.
3 · People joiningWho is in and what they have done appears above the blocks. Chasing is named for the first time — and Roam has started, so it says when the next reminder goes.
4 · ChasingWhat is outstanding, in the order it was drawn in the first mock-up, with the roster underneath and the four blocks folded at the bottom.

The rule

  • Never report on work nobody has been asked to do. Outstanding means somebody was asked and has not done it; before that it is just a list.
  • Stage 3 is where the app has to be at its most encouraging: two people in, twenty-two to go, and the thing that helps is the code and the WhatsApp button, not a red 22.

What is already built and stays

  • The chase blocks, the roster, the person view, the reminder schedule and its honesty about not being able to send.
  • The participant's list, joining from a link, self-declared bookings, the wrong-weekend catch.
  • What changes is the order, the empty states, and the money model underneath block 4.

What it costs to change

  • Blocks 1–3: a reordering of the panel that exists, plus a QR (a small dependency, or ~60 lines) and a wa.me link. Half a day.
  • Block 4: real work — a charges list with three pricing shapes, per-head/per-party, a minimum, and the arithmetic that follows a moving price. A day, and worth it: it is the part nobody else does.

6 · The calls to make

QuestionWhat I would doWhy
Wizard or page?B — one page of four numbered blocks, opening in order the first time and staying a page after.The first run needs a wizard's discipline; every run after it needs a settings page. B is both without two screens to build or two states to keep in step.
Is the money a list or a fee?A list. The coach and the tickets are two charges with different shapes on the same trip.A single fee cannot describe the ordinary case, and an organiser who has to add them together by hand is back in the spreadsheet.
Which pricing shapes in v1?Fixed each and split a total. Bands can wait — the ladder is the same code, so it is a fortnight's work later rather than a rewrite.Those two cover a ticket and a coach, which is nearly everything. Bands add explaining that a first version does not need.
Per head by default?Per head, and say what it means for the party in front of them: “Priya + Leo = £66.68”.Coaches, dinners and tickets are per head. A room is the exception, and it is a booking rather than a charge.
A minimum, in v1?Yes — a number and a date, shown to everyone before they pay, and said in the reminders while it is short.It is the owner's question, it costs almost nothing once a charge has a headcount, and it is the difference between an organiser who commits and one who cannot.
WhatsApp now, or next?Sharing now (a wa.me link, no integration), reminders later (the Business API).The first is a link and works today. The second is a verified sender, approved templates and a per-message cost.
Do we say it is a group trip before it is set up?Yes, but with what is missing: “Nobody asked in yet.”Half a group is worse than none if the app implies people have been asked.

The one-paragraph version: the Group tab opens on four numbered questions instead of a progress report — what is wanted from everyone and what is only being asked about, a code big enough to point a phone at with a link and a WhatsApp button beside it, how often Roam chases, and what you are charging for. That last one becomes a list of charges rather than a fee, each priced either as the same amount for everybody or as a total split between whoever comes, per head or per party, with a minimum number for it to happen at all and a date after which it is not refundable. Chasing does not appear at all until somebody has joined.
