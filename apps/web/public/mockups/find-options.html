Find, to the style guide

The same three directions, redrawn to the Roam colour and style guidelines v1 (September 2026): white ground with one mint header field in light mode, charcoal in dark; ink buttons in light, mint in dark; icons one colour per mode; red for the heart only; no gradients, no per-row colour; Archivo throughout, Caveat for the wordmark only; 4px corners, pills for chips. Every screen is shown in both modes. Sibling pages: Shortlist → Trip · Trips tab.
