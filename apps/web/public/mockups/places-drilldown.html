The rules on this screen

  • Countries → cities → the city. One country in the atlas: the country row is skipped and the cities are the first screen.
  • No trips inside a city. Trips live in Trips. "Plan a trip here" stays as the one link across, top right.
  • Top row: Everything · Things to do · Food & drink. Each shows its count for the city.
  • Two dropdowns under it. Status: Any, Been, To try, Special. Type: the kinds of thing in the segment you're on — museums, parks & playgrounds, theatre & shows, pubs & bars, clubs & live music… or cuisines under Food & drink. Both show counts and update each other.
  • Pubs. "Pubs & bars" is a type under Things to do; a pub that serves food is also under Food & drink as "Pub food". So The Churchill Arms shows in both, once each. Clubs and live music are a type of their own, for the older ones.
  • List or Map. A toggle at the right of the segment row. Map fills the screen; tap a pin for the place; the same filters apply to the pins. No map under the list.
  • Add a place sits with the title. Search by name, for a place you already know.
