Plan screen — three ways, and how it becomes a trip

Every option keeps the rules that already hold: the box comes first and is big; Speak and Send sit under it; Who's coming is one line ("The family") that opens to ticks; nothing is guessed — anything the planner is not sure of gets a ? and a question with answers you can tap or say. The three differ in where the criteria live and how "Inspire me" fits. The last section shows the same journey end to end: speaking → criteria filling in → one question → the trip.

Option A · Two modes: "Tell me" / "Inspire me"

A switch under the title. Tell me is what exists today, with a "So far" card that fills as you speak. Inspire me is a mood board: pick a few pills, cap the travel, and get a list of ideas — each one becomes a plan with one tap.

A2 · Inspire me. Pills, a travel cap, and ideas that name why (who likes what, how far). "Plan this" drops the idea into Tell me with the criteria already filled.

What it optimises

  • Two clear jobs, one switch. The suggestions under the box today become a real place to go.
  • "So far" reads like a receipt: you can watch it get things right.

Trade-offs

  • Two panels on one screen while speaking; on a small phone "So far" is below the fold until Done.
  • Inspire me needs a small model call to make ideas from the pills + the atlas.

C2 · Empty state: the box, then Inspire me pills in place of today's three random sentences. They disappear once you start.

What it optimises

  • Smallest change from what is live; the chat and its questions stay as they are.
  • The strip fits one line on a phone and is tappable — tap "Arrive ?" to answer it.

Trade-offs

  • Chips are terse; the full row view (Option B) is clearer for numbers like "2 to eat, 3 to do".
  • Inspire me is a card, not a mode: fine for a first version, cramped if it grows.

Side by side

A · Two modes

  • Best if Inspire me is a first-class thing you use often.
  • "So far" card = receipt.

B · Rows are the screen

  • Best for confidence while speaking: each fact lands in its slot with ✓ / ?.
  • Tap-to-edit any row; the chat shrinks to one line.

C · Chat + summary strip

  • Least change; keeps today's questions-with-chips.
  • Strip is compact but terse.

Common to all

  • Box first, big; Speak/Send under it.
  • Who's coming = "The family ▸", opens to ticks.
  • Suggestions gone once you start.
  • No guessing: ? + a question.
  • Set-up card before the trip opens.
